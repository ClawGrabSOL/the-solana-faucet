What are Solanas?

Solanas are a new kind of money. They aren't created or controlled by a government (like dollars or euros). Anybody who wants to can run the software and be part of the network. Visit solana.com for the geeky details.

How do I get a Solana Receiving Address?

Install a wallet such as Phantom or Solflare (or use the Solana CLI if you live in a terminal). Open it up. It will show you your receiving address, a long string of letters and numbers. Paste that here. A wallet you control is best.
